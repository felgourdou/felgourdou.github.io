From PIA to France 2030: Evaluating Competitive Support for Firm Growth

with Philippe Aghion, Simon Bunel, Xavier Jaravel, and Alexandra Roulet

French Council of Economic Analysis (CAE)

download

This Focus provides a causal evaluation of competitive support granted by Bpifrance to firms under the PIA programs and France 2030. Using a new panel that matches applications filed since 2010 with administrative firm data, it compares funded firms with non-selected applicants within the same calls for projects. The results show persistent gains in employment, research effort, patenting, capital accumulation, sales, and value added. Additional tax and social security revenues generated by funded firms exceed the committed public support within three years, although the findings apply only to competitively awarded funding.

2024

Evaluation of the Impact of France 2030 on the Decarbonization of the Economy

French Environment and Energy Management Agency (ADEME)

download

This public summary assesses how France 2030 contributes to decarbonizing the French economy. It reviews the first stage of deployment, characterizes supported decarbonization solutions, examines impact measurement practices for greenhouse-gas reductions, and evaluates the strategic positioning of the project portfolio.
